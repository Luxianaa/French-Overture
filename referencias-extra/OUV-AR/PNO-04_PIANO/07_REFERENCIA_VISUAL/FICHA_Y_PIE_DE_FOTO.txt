Título: François I Brandishing a Sword
Autor(es): Alexandre-Evariste Fragonard (French, 1781–1850)
Fecha: c. 1815–30
Técnica: Pen and brown ink and brush and brown wash with graphite underdrawing and white and yellow gouache heightening on wove paper
